Heartbeat (optional)

If the whole VM or its host goes down nothing inside it can alert you. Give a monitoring service a push/ping URL and TimeCanvas calls it every minute; that service messages you when the calls stop. Works with healthchecks.io or Uptime Kuma (a Push monitor, for example http://100.x.x.x:3001/api/push/<token>?status=up&msg=OK, including on your tailnet).
